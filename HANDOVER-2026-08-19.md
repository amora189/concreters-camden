# PART 1 — Plain English

## What this is

A WordPress site for a concreting business targeting Camden and South West Sydney, NSW, at `concreterscamden.com.au`. It was built by transforming an existing Melbourne site (`bestconcretersmelbourne.com.au`, "E&T Co Concreters") into a NSW one, keeping the Elementor design and replacing everything else.

Trading name is now **Structure Co Concreters Camden**.

## Where it actually stands

**Nothing is live. Zero pages are publishable. Index-ready is 0.** The site exists as an XML export file plus supporting artifacts. Nothing has been imported, deployed, or indexed.

## What went wrong, and what it cost

Three findings reshaped the project:

**82.4% of the writing is machine-generated nonsense.** Sentences like *"new-driveway scope records scope boundary; new-driveway scope identifies the record owner."* A coherence scan found 90 SEVERE pages — every service page, every guide, every intersection, every cost page — plus 49 stilted-but-real suburb pages. Only 5 pages scored clean. The 15 original validation gates never caught it because they measured structure, uniqueness and meta length, never meaning. Uniqueness in fact scored these pages *highest*, because the filler varies per page.

Consequence: **81 pages withdrawn.** 157 planned → 77 active.

**Twenty images were Melbourne photos renamed to claim NSW places.** `TARNEIT-SOIL.jpg` → `wianamatta-shale-clay-camden-1020.jpg`, sitting on 15 pages presenting Victorian basalt-plains soil as Camden's Wianamatta shale. Plus three AI-generated images, a fake VERIFIED badge on 14 pages, and a favicon with the words "E & T Co — CONCRETERS MELBOURNE" drawn into it.

**The business identity does not exist.** No ABN, no NSW Fair Trading licence, no insurance, no verified legal entity, no signed licensed NSW operator. Consequence: all 105 `Service` schema nodes omit their provider, no `LocalBusiness` schema can be emitted, and the privacy policy names no accountable entity.
