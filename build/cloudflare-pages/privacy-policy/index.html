Operating model Structure Co Concreters Camden manages concreting enquiries and coordinates suitable independent providers. Job-specific quotations, contractual terms, licensing, insurance and warranty information must be confirmed before work begins. Submitting an enquiry does not create a construction contract. What is collected The planned enquiry form may collect a name, phone number, suburb, requested service, optional email address, approximate job size and a free-text message. It must not collect payment details. Why it is collected and disclosed Information is used to respond to the enquiry and, with the submitter's consent, may be shared with a suitable independent provider so that the provider can assess the enquiry. It is not sold, rented or disclosed for third-party marketing. Delivery, storage and retention Form submissions are to be delivered to info@concreterscamden.com.au.
