What to include

A practical brief can be simple.

  • Property address or locality
  • Intended use and approximate dimensions
  • Existing surface, access and drainage notes
  • Relevant drawings or Council correspondence
  • Preferred timing and any constraints
i

What happens next?

We review the information and coordinate the next step where a suitable independent provider can assess the scope. Provider identity, quotation, contract, licensing, insurance and warranty details must be confirmed before work begins.

Submitting an enquiry does not create a construction contract.
